Research

03 — thesis & applied ML

SimXGNN: Similarity-Enhanced Cross-Layered GNN for DTI Prediction

PES University

Designed a novel cross-layered GNN combining GCN, GAT, and GraphSAGE within a unified architecture to capture multi-scale molecular representations for drug–target interaction prediction. Constructed a heterogeneous multi-source graph integrating 12,015 nodes from BindingDB, DrugBank, UniProt, and AlphaFold; applied contrastive learning and adaptive sampling to handle extreme class imbalance. Designed edge-aware message-passing mechanisms and analysed learned attention weights to identify biologically meaningful interaction patterns.
